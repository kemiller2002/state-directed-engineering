## Adversarial review

Three revision passes produced material changes:

1. **Traceability:** a REQUIRED adaptive-telemetry rule initially lacked a row
   in `doctrine/EVIDENCE-TO-ENGINEERING-MAP.md`; the row was added.
2. **Ceremony/drift:** feature manifests were optional for justified
   small/obvious areas in their own contract but unconditional in execution
   flows; all routes now honor the semantic map's explicit not-needed entry.
3. **Representation mismatch:** the method allows several site-level change
   classes while templates forced one singular class; templates now accept a
   list and require site-level explanation.

A fourth sweep checked stale terminology, global-search priority, compiler
overclaiming, manifest mandates, multi-class fields, frozen paths, whitespace,
and local links. It produced no additional content change. Further speculative
revision had diminishing value, so the method's stop condition applied.
